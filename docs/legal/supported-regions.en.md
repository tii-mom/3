# Supported Countries and Regions

Last updated: September 13, 2026

## 1. The General Rule

We only serve regions where **local law permits** use of the relevant AI services. Before ordering, please check the compliance requirements in your own region.

## 2. Where We Don't Serve

We don't offer service — and won't accept orders — in these cases:

- Countries and regions under international sanctions or export-control restrictions;
- Countries and regions that explicitly prohibit ChatGPT / OpenAI services;
- Regions where payment providers restrict us and settlement isn't possible.

## 3. Not Sure?

If you're unsure whether you can use the service, **ask support first — don't pay first**. If a line is crossed, the only thing we can do is cancel and refund.

## 4. Notes

Our coverage follows changes in local policies and upstream providers' rules. Whatever this page says at the time is what applies.
