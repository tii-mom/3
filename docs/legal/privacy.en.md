# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Thank you for using 3API (hereinafter "the Service"). We understand the importance of your personal information and are committed to protecting your privacy with a prudent, minimal-collection approach. This policy explains what we collect, how we use, store, and protect your information when you use our AI top-up, finished-account, and API relay services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1. Account & Identity
- Email address or phone number provided at registration or login;
- Verification information necessary for risk control and security.

### 2. Orders & Payments
- The product specifications you choose, order history, and order status;
- Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Alipay. We **do not store your card number, password, or payment PIN**, retaining only the transaction ID, amount, and payment status needed for reconciliation and after-sales support.

### 3. Information Required for Fulfillment
- ChatGPT account session credentials or account information you voluntarily provide for top-ups or account delivery, used solely to complete the service you purchased and never for any other purpose;
- API relay call logs (model called, token usage, call time) used for billing, troubleshooting, and risk control.

### 4. Technical & Security Information
- Device model, IP address, browser type, and access time, used for security, anti-fraud, and experience optimization.

## 2. How We Use Information
- Fulfilling orders and delivering products and services;
- Processing payments and issuing records;
- Providing customer support and after-sales service;
- Risk control, anti-abuse, and security;
- Improving the product and service (never for intrusive targeted advertising).

## 3. Storage & Security
- Encryption is applied in transit and at rest;
- Sensitive credentials are accessed only for the time strictly necessary to fulfill the service, then cleaned up under a minimization principle;
- Access controls and audit logging are in place to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or tampering.

## 4. Information Sharing
- **We do not sell** your personal information;
- We share only the minimum necessary information with payment providers (e.g., Alipay) to complete transactions;
- When required by law or to protect the Service and its users, we may disclose relevant information to authorized authorities in accordance with applicable regulations.

## 5. Your Rights
You may, at any time:
- Access and correct your account information;
- Request export or deletion of your personal data (except where retention is required by law);
- Withdraw non-essential consents.

To exercise these rights, contact us through the Service's support channel.

## 6. Cookies & Similar Technologies
We may use cookies to maintain login state and security. You can manage or clear these cookies through your browser settings.

## 7. Minor Protection
The Service is primarily intended for adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors; if we become aware of minor usage, we will handle it under a minimization principle and, where appropriate, contact a guardian.

## 8. Policy Updates
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via an in-service notice or message; the latest version always prevails.

## 9. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out through the Service's support channel.
