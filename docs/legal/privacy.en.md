# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The one-liner: we only collect what's needed to finish the job, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for anything else.

## 1. What We Collect

- **Contact details**: the phone number or email you enter at checkout, used to look up your order and provide after-sales support;
- **Order information**: what you bought, what it cost, and how far along the order is;
- **Top-up credentials**: the ChatGPT session credential you provide, used only to complete that specific top-up;
- **Usage records**: API relay call logs (which model, how many tokens, when), used for billing and troubleshooting;
- **Basic device info**: IP address, browser type, and the like, used for anti-abuse and security.

## 2. The Money Part

Payments run through third-party providers such as Alipay. We **do not store your card number or payment password** — only the transaction ID and amount, for reconciliation and after-sales support.

## 3. How We Use It

- Fulfilling orders, delivering, and supporting you afterward;
- Billing and issuing records;
- Anti-abuse, anti-fraud, and security;
- Making the product better. Never for spammy advertising.

## 4. Do We Share It?

- **We don't sell it**;
- Only the minimal information payment providers need goes to them;
- If the law requires it, or to keep you and everyone else safe, we may disclose information to authorized authorities as required by law.

## 5. What You Can Do

- View and correct your account information;
- Ask us to export or delete your data (except where the law requires us to keep it);
- Withdraw consents you don't need.

Just message support.

## 6. Cookies

Used to keep you logged in and to protect the service. You can clear them in your browser settings.

## 7. Minors

The service is aimed at adults. We don't knowingly collect information from minors; if we learn of any, we'll handle it under a minimization principle.

## 8. Policy Updates

We'll announce changes through an in-site notice. The latest version always prevails.

## 9. Contact Us

Questions? Reach us through the in-site support channel.
